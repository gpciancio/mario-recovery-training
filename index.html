Your timeline

Build back gradually.

Recovery · Nov 20–Dec 1

Up to 20–30% of customary weights, one easy set. Three resistance days only with clinic permission; two low-intensity recovery days. Keep effort no higher than 3/10.

Week 5 · Dec 2–8

After clearance, roughly 40–50% of customary loads and two sets, leaving 4–5 repetitions in reserve. Avoid abrupt increases and strenuous pickleball until cleared.

Week 6 · Dec 9–15

If symptom-free and cleared to progress, roughly 60–70% loads and two or three sets. Never jump straight to failure or maximum weights.

Saturday and Sunday: rest or comfortable walking. The plan is a conservative training suggestion and does not replace your surgeon's postoperative instructions.
